# Page Builder

SURFACES is the one place pages are made. It replaces the old BUTTONS, PAGES and Control Devices tabs.

## Do it

1. Drag a button from the shared library onto a slot. The builder refuses drops that break the four-zone grammar and tells you why.
2. Edits go live on the USB deck and in Companion right away. There is no SYNC step. Undo also goes live.
3. Control Center tags the Companion pages it owns, adds new ones after your last hand-built page, and only rewrites pages that still carry its tag.
