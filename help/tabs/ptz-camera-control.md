# Riding a camera

This tab is one camera's RCP: lens, exposure, paint and presets for the camera picked at the top. The Stream Deck, the TourBox and this tab always follow the same selected camera.

## Do it

1. Pick the camera at the top, or press its CAM key on the deck.
2. Turn the iris knob. Slow turns are fine steps; fast spins are big steps.
3. Press the knob to flip between IRIS and GAIN (R and B on the white knobs).
4. Hold the knob 800 ms to reset to the camera's scene value. That is blocked while this camera is on air.
5. Store a preset with its scope; recall uses the scope it was saved with.

A live camera's controls keep their colour and get a red frame plus a LIVE corner. The camera's own key still goes solid red.
