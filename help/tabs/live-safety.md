# Live safety

When a camera or source is on program, its target key goes solid red. The controls that ride it keep their job colour and show a red frame plus a LIVE corner — that is enough.

## Do it

1. Exposure reset (a long press of 800 ms) is blocked on a live camera. Turning still works.
2. Paint LOCK on the STATUS column stops every paint knob and shows LOCKED.
3. Panel lock (CONSOLE LOCK) stops the deck from sending. A refused press flashes amber.
