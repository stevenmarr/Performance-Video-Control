# HOME

HOME is the deck's front door. Systems on the desk sit on the TARGET row, in desk order. A system with something on program turns solid red. Press it to open that system's first page.

## Do it

1. CUES, PRESETS, CONSOLE and SELF-TEST are on the function row.
2. ON AIR, LOCK, LINK and ALERTS stay on the right.
3. The knobs are unbound — HOME is not a paint page.
4. Adding or removing a system updates the TARGET row and moves nothing else.
